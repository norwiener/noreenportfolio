Glitch Art | Silly Cats! :3

Yooo these are my glitched cats! I used Notepad++ and Audacity for the cats. These cats don't stop staring... MEOWWW!!!

The three photos I chose are of silly cats that I like. The first cat is my favorite; her name is Milly, and she is the real "BLEHHH" cat. The second cat is Rigby; she's making a similar facial expression to Milly, but Rigby is known for her fainting goat syndrome, where muscles cannot relax normally right after contractin. The third cat is Zazu; he is known as the "Thousand Yard Stare Cat". I still wanted his stare to be captured even while glitched. The cat's expressions go from the silliest to the most serious; geeked to locked in.
